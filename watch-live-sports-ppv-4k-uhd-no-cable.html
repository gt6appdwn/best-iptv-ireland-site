  3. Connect the device. Plug the streaming device into an HDMI‑2.0 (or higher) port. Ensure the TV input is set to the correct HDMI source.
  4. Install the Best IPTV Ireland app. Open the device’s app store, search for ‘Best IPTV Ireland’, and install the official application.
  5. Enter your subscription details. Launch the app, sign in with the credentials sent to your email after purchase, and activate the service.
  6. Configure video settings. In the app settings, select ‘4K UHD’ as the default resolution. Enable ‘HDR’ if your TV supports it.
  7. Test with a free channel. Open a live channel labeled ‘4K Test’ to verify picture quality and buffering performance.
  8. Purchase PPV events. Navigate to the ‘PPV’ section, select the upcoming fight or match, and complete the payment using a secure gateway.

Troubleshooting Common Issues

If you encounter buffering, pixelation, or login problems, try the following fixes before contacting support.

  • Buffering in 4K. Reduce the stream quality to 1080p temporarily, then switch back after a few minutes.
  • No sound. Verify that the TV’s audio output is set to HDMI ARC or optical, and that the app’s volume is not muted.
  • App crashes. Clear the app cache from the device settings and ensure the app is updated to the latest version.
  • Login fails. Double‑check the email address and password, and reset the password if needed.
  • PPV purchase not reflected. Refresh the ‘My Events’ page; if the issue persists, contact support with the transaction ID.

Best Practices for a Seamless Experience

To keep your sports streaming smooth, consider these habits:

  1. Use a wired Ethernet connection whenever possible; Wi‑Fi can introduce interference.
  2. Place the router in an open area and avoid obstacles that block the signal.
  3. Schedule firmware updates for your streaming device during off‑peak hours.
  4. Enable ‘Do Not Disturb’ on the TV to prevent pop‑ups during live events.
